# Aryan Saha


I'm a student at Princeton studying a mix of computer engineering, chemistry, and applied physics. I'm 
interested in making computers learn science, and how to leverage these tools to accelerate discovery.

I'm part of the [Rosen Research Group](https://rosen.cbe.princeton.edu), where we focus on high-throughput computing and quantum-chemical calculations. My research focuses
on using DFT calculations, graph learning, diffusion models, and more to help analyze and predict properties of unique materials. 

At Princeton, I help run [Princeton Student Ventures](https://www.psv.vc) where we give $5-10k grants to student founders, 
and [Princeton Biotechnology Group](https://biotech.princeton.edu) where we host speakers in biotech. I'm grateful to be supported by the [Masason Foundation](https://masason-foundation.org/en/) 
as an Associate Member.

I'm always happy to chat! Reach out to aryansaha [at] princeton.edu
